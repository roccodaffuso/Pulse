# Pulse Support

Last updated: 2026-09-03

Use [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/roccodaffuso/Pulse/issues) for product
support, bug reports, and feature requests.

Pulse is maintained by Rocco D'Affuso. For a private question or if you cannot
use GitHub Issues, contact
[roccodaffuso@gmail.com](mailto:roccodaffuso@gmail.com). Do not send Health
values, medical information, personal identifiers, or sensitive screenshots by
email.

## Helpful Details

When reporting a problem, include:

- The Pulse version and build shown in Settings.
- Your iOS or watchOS version.
- The affected surface: iPhone app, Apple Watch app, widget, or complication.
- Clear steps that reproduce the problem.
- What you expected and what happened instead.

## Protect Your Privacy

GitHub Issues are public. Do not post:

- Health values or medical information.
- Personal contact details or identifiers.
- Screenshots containing sensitive information.
- Unreviewed debug reports.

Pulse is not a medical device and support cannot provide medical advice.
